Service Details

Inventory asset management refers to any management or tracking of internal office supplies, properties, systems, machinery that are crucial for day to day business operations. Typically assets are associated with a high value, so it is really important to keep a track of all incoming and outgoing assets. Being a crucial part of any business, assets directly or indirectly help in generating revenues and increasing the business value. Also, every asset has a commercial value and can be used in current or future to generate revenues for the organization.

To keep your assets up to date organizations have to involve in regular repair and maintenance activities. The other major challenge for organizations is the cost associated with asset repair, legal compliance for operations, theft of parts, and overall higher maintenance budgets. Not having a good asset management system can increase the complexity and costings of asset repair and maintenance. These hidden costs, directly and indirectly, affect the growth and ROI of the organization.

BusinessX Inventory Asset Management offers a wide range of features to track and manage the asset inventory, like Asset depreciation, Repair logs, Maintenance schedules, operating instructions, thus resulting in cost optimization and higher asset life.

To manage the business an organization requires access to in-depth, real-time data to assist in improving decisions. BusinessX ERP provides detailed analysis with the help of Machine Learning (ML) and Business Intelligence (BI). Whereas Machine Learning (ML) is used to actively learn from imported and gathered data, in a way as people would use past experiences as a part of their process to plan a better strategy for the organization.

Business intelligence (BI), on the other side, is a complex sphere representing a process that depends on automation to cumulate, store and analyze business-related data. The goal of BI is to succeed in optimal courses of action in a short time as possible, therefore the process includes several different aspects, like analytics, predictive modelling, performance management, data processing, etc.
