Can I get a demo of BusinessX Software?

Yes, we would be happy to demonstrate BusinessX software through a web-conference at your convenience. The demo is a one-hour session. To schedule your personal session, please contact sales@kineticxtech.com.

What types of payment do you accept?

We accept payment via Visa, MasterCard, American Express and PayPal. We also accept payment via bank transfer or check transfer for yearly subscriptions. For more details, please contact sales@kineticxtech.com.

Does BusinessX support multiple languages?

Yes, we support all international languages: English (US), English (UK), Finnish, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Portugal), Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Swedish, Thai, Chinese (China), Chinese (Taiwan), Danish, Dutch (Netherlands), Polish, Hungarian, Turkish, Vietnamese, Croatian, Czech and many more.

How long are your contracts?

You are not bound by any contracts or commitments. BusinessX software is a pay-as-you-go service, so you can make changes or upgrade your plan requirements whenever you need to.

Is my data safe?

We’re fervent about keeping your data safe and secure. Our facilities feature stringent 24/7/365 security with video monitoring, biometric access, and advanced fire, flood, and theft monitoring systems. Our network security system employs the latest encryption and intrusion detection and prevention technologies.

Can I switch or upgrade plans?

Of course, you can! Go to Contact Us Page on our website, and follow the steps on the contact us page to get a call back from us.
