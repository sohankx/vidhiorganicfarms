Know About Our Farm

Vidhi Amrit Farm is spread over many acres in the district of Nohar Hanumanghar (Rajasthan) The farm is equipped with the finest international technology for feeding, milking, and processing. We follow the traditional approach and offer genuine and organic quality of Desi Ghee. We supply Bilona Ghee which is 100% organic, A2, and prepared by the Vedic Bilona method. Moreover, we supply Gir Cow Ghee to our clients all over India. We ensure purest quality ghee packed in cleaned glass bottles. We have the best breeds of Desi Cows like Sahiwal, Gir, Tharparkar, & Rathi. We don’t give them hormonal injections and feed only self-grown organic grass and are known for providing farm-to-door delivery. As one of the preeminent Vedic A2 Ghee Suppliers in Hanumangarh, we ensure the availability of the most hygienic, purest, and quality ghee for end customers.

Milk

We pamper our cows just the way one ought to pamper their own child.

Our milk is naturally procured from cows that graze freely, openly, and are raised healthily on a farm. None of our cows are tied up. Milk isn’t forced from the cows and is extracted only after the calf has had sufficient milk. We treat our cows with ayurvedic medicinal herbs, like shankhpushpi, ajwain, Shatavari, ashwagandha, etc. We give the cows regular neem sprays to keep diseases away from them. We spread limestone on the floors of their sheds, which helps them walk, sit or sleep comfortably. Non-milking cows, calves, and bulls are treated the same way as milking cows without any discrimination.
